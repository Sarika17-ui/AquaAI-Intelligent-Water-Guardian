      Mission

      About Jal Raksha

      Purpose

      Jal Raksha is designed to help municipal teams and water operators identify unusual water-quality changes before they become operational issues requiring inspection and lab confirmation.

      Monitoring philosophy

      The system focuses on anomaly detection, trend analysis and explainable alerts. It does not claim to identify every potential source or guarantee water safety from sensor data alone.

      Hardware readiness

      Demo Data is simulated. Hardware Mode is shown as not connected until an ESP32/API provider is available. This prototype simulates pH and dissolved oxygen; it does not claim the physical prototype measures dissolved oxygen.
